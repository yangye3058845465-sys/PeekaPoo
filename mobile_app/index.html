    How alerts work

    1. Personal baseline. Gas readings are compared with your own last 14 days, so your normal is the reference.
    2. Persistence rule. A single odd day never alerts. A signal must stay unusual for 3 days in a row.
    3. Fixed rules decide the level. Normal / Watch / Consult comes from deterministic rules, and the AI only explains it.
